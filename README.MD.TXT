Fashion Sales & Customer Market Intelligence
Business Case
A growing fashion company sells clothing, footwear, and accessories through online and physical retail channels.

Business Problem
Some products perform well while others have low sales or remain in inventory for long periods.

Main Business Question
What products, customers, seasons, regions, and sales channels drive revenue, and how can the company improve sales and inventory strategy?

Dataset Description
The dataset contains fashion retail sales transactions, customer information, product details, regions, sales channels, pricing, and discounts.

Key Findings
High-performing categories generated most revenue.
Certain regions outperformed others.
Seasonal demand affected purchasing patterns.
Discounts influenced sales volume.
Business Recommendations
Increase inventory for top-performing categories.
Focus marketing on high-revenue products.
Stock popular sizes and colors.
Improve low-performing products.
Plan inventory based on seasonal demand.
Conclusion
Data-driven inventory planning, marketing, and seasonal forecasting can improve revenue and profitability.
