Fashion Boutique Sales Analysis
Project Overview
This capstone project focuses on analyzing a Fashion Boutique Dataset to uncover insights into product distribution, inventory value, customer ratings, colors, sizes, and brand performance.

The goal of this analysis is to help fashion retailers make data-driven decisions regarding inventory management, product planning, and business strategy.

Business Problem
Fashion retailers need to understand how products are distributed across categories, brands, sizes, and colors while identifying high-value inventory categories.

This analysis seeks to answer the following questions:

Which product categories contain the most products?
Which brands have the highest inventory counts?
Which categories generate the highest inventory value?
What product sizes and colors are most common?
How are customer ratings distributed?

Key Findings
1. Product Categories
Accessories contain the highest number of products.
Dresses contain the lowest number of products.
2. Brand Distribution
Zara has the highest product count.
Other brands show relatively balanced product representation.
3. Inventory Value
Outerwear has the highest inventory value.
Shoes have the second-highest inventory value.
Accessories have the lowest inventory value despite having the largest number of products.
4. Product Sizes
Unknown size appears most frequently.
XS and S are the most common known sizes.
5. Product Colors
Green is the most represented color.
Black and Red are also highly represented.
6. Customer Ratings
Most customer ratings are concentrated around 3 out of 5.
Customer satisfaction appears moderate overall.
Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis:

Prioritize inventory management for Outerwear and Shoes because they contribute the highest inventory value.

Improve product size data quality to reduce the number of Unknown size entries.

Monitor customer feedback and ratings to improve customer satisfaction.

Use category-level performance insights when planning future inventory investments.

Continue tracking product trends to optimize inventory and marketing strategies.
